# Grow Room Sessions
## Whitepaper · public developer edition

**November 25, 2026 · Audio edition and listening page**

### Goal and audience

Give the Grow Room Version of The Soil Remembers a canonical listening copy. The immediate audience is listeners discovering POTIFY through the soundtrack.

### Design and implementation

A standalone player references the recording extracted from the First Cycle edition. The source hash distinguishes this track from old intro revisions. The edition-specific master is the reference for trailers and listening links. Giving the track a stable filename and digest makes an accidental music substitution detectable.

### What a contributor can establish

The public entry page returned HTTP 200 and rendered local content in Chromium at a 390 × 844 viewport. Test playback from a cold visit and manually check the edit point before attaching it to a film. A hash confirms the file, not how an edit sounds.

### Evidence and outstanding work

A radio, streaming or sync license requires specific rights and terms. Listening is free in this edition. The next acceptance case is concrete. Press play on the named track and check the same file hash against the edition master.

### Place in POTIFY

Seek one expressed interest in music licensing or a physical edition subject to the recording's rights. The marketing plan gives the test window and the evidence for a commercial outcome.
