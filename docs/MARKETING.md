# Grow Room Sessions marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Listeners discovering POTIFY through the soundtrack. **Offer.** Give the Grow Room Version of The Soil Remembers a canonical listening copy.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Share a short captioned listening clip that leads to the correctly labeled recording. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Play the Grow Room Version. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 10 qualified listening starts. |

### Sales test

Validate one licensing inquiry or three requests for a soundtrack edition. A license sale needs signed terms and clearance for the intended use. Seek one expressed interest in music licensing or a physical edition subject to the recording's rights.

### Measurement and decision

Use `grow-room-sessions-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** A radio, streaming or sync license requires specific rights and terms. Listening is free in this edition. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
